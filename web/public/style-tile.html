Design system, on one page.

This is the style tile for MemoryLoom tokens v2. It is not part of the product and is not linked from the product navigation. Every specimen below is rendered from tokens.css alone, so what you see is exactly what the landing page and the console will adopt.

How to judge it: hold the page at arm’s length. It should read as one ink on one paper, set in three voices — a serif for headlines, a plain text face for reading, a mono face for data. Colour should be almost absent. The single accent appears once on this page, in Figure 1, where it marks the one place a decision is live. The small chips in the colour table are specimens, labelled with their values; they are documentation, not decoration.

01 — Typography

Three voices, each with one job

Headlines speak in the display serif. Reading speaks in the text face. Anything the system recorded — numbers, receipts, labels — speaks in mono. If a line’s job is unclear, its face is wrong.

An agent proposes a memory; it never writes directly. A decision gate scores the proposal and answers with one of three words — approve, reject, or escalate — and escalate always exists. Approved memories land in the truth store with their provenance attached, so a later reader can always ask why the team believes something, and get an answer from the record rather than from memory of memory.

04 — Motion

Sophisticated, or absent

Three timings exist: 120ms for hover and focus feedback, 240ms for state changes, 600ms for a reveal that happens once. Motion is a status channel — it reports that something changed — never decoration. Under reduced-motion settings every animation on this page is switched off and content is simply present.

A reveal, once

This card faded and settled by eight pixels as it entered the viewport — the only entrance motion the system allows. Scroll away and back: it does not repeat. If your system asks for reduced motion, it never moved at all.

The one ambient motion in the product — the memory graph’s playhead — is driven by the data itself: it sweeps the corpus timeline and memories appear at the moment they were recorded. It can be paused, and it stops under reduced motion.
